Traditional Delicacies

Samoan cuisine is defined by the Umu (earth oven) and fresh tropical staples like coconut, taro, and fish.

Oka I’a (Raw Fish Salad)

Oka is a staple raw fish salad made with fresh tuna or snapper marinated in citrus juice and thick coconut cream. Unlike other ceviches, it is known for its rich, creamy texture and is often mixed with crunchy cucumbers and onions.

Palusami

Considered the heart of the Sunday To’ona’i feast, Palusami consists of young taro leaves with seasoned coconut cream and onions, wrapped into bundles, and baked until the cream becomes a savory custard.

Panipopo (Coconut Buns)

Samoa's beloved national dessert. These fluffy sweet buns are baked in a generous amount of sweetened coconut milk, resulting in a sticky, custard-like bottom and a soft, golden top. This is me and lots of my familys favorite dessert as the sweet coconut cream with the hot golden buns makes for a warm and satisfying treat.

Sapasui (Samoan Chop Suey)

A popular dish introduced to Samoa featuring vermicelli glass noodles stir-fried with soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and meat (usually pork or beef, for my family we prefer beef). It is an essential party food found at almost every gathering.
