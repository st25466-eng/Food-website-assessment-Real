Why This Page Exists

This website was created to preserve and celebrate the rich culinary history of Samoa. In a fast-paced world, the traditional methods of the Umu are sometimes forgotten. I just wanted to let people know about the enriched culture of Samoa and the foods that define it.

The Story Behind It

Growing up in New Zealand, I've never had much knowledge about my culture and history around it. This project started as a way to document my cultures recipes and the cultural significance of the Sunday feast (To’ona’i). I want to share that warmth with the world. By showing off the cultural food creations.

My Personal Connection

Food has always been a way for me to connect with my heritage. The smells, tastes, and communal nature of Samoan meals evoke a deep sense of belonging and nostalgia. Whenever I gather with family and friends to share a meal, I feel a great connection to my roots and the traditions that have been passed down through generations. This website is a tribute to those cherished moments and the vibrant culture that shapes them.
